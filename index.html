huu's personal website

Welcome!

Hi! I'm huu.
I'm a 27 year old retro game enthusiast, a retired YouTuber and currently an IT engineer by trade. I know this website seems basic for someone like me but I've had this site since 2014 more or less & want to avoid changing its "authenticity" if you get what I mean.
What this website is for:
The goal of this website is to basically serve as an archive for:
  • Abandonware games that are hard to find but I happen to have backups for;
    • Currently hosting the following games:
      • Hexis;
      • Safari Biathlon Racer;
  • A directory for lost music that I have recovered;
    • The 2013-2015 Ongaku Shoujo discography;
    • Halozy's "H -Heart and Beat Technology-" album;
  • An osu! content archive for:
    • My own skins throughout the years;
      • Including other people's skins that I've historically used that have since become impossible to find;
    • My own beatmaps throughout the years that have been lost;
    • Other people's beatmaps that have become lost media;
      • This includes Ranked beatmaps that are not available to download from the official osu! website;
      • and deleted beatmaps from mappers that have essentially become pretty much lost;
  • A personal Open Hexagon level pack archive for level packs that I have created in 2014-2016.
