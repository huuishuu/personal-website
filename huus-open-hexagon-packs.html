My Open Hexagon v1.92 level packs

This is a page where you can download all my Open Hexagon level packs for version 1.92 of the game, made from 2014 to 2016.

You can find a download for v1.92 of Open Hexagon on GitHub by clicking here.

Please keep in mind that, depending on your antivirus, the executable for Open Hexagon 1.92 could be filtered as malicious. If you're having trouble starting this version of the game, add it to your antivirus exceptions.
Known bugs with the level packs

All level packs
  • Due to RAM limitations - levels using hardpatterns.lua may load an unavoidable, fully closed hexagon at times.
  • The timer will start counting up very slowly past 100 seconds (unfixable issue if you're on a modern OS)

Super Hexagon for Open Hexagon (SHfOH)
  • The sound "awesome.ogg" may not play when needed.
  • If you play the pack with 3D Effect: On, then the shadows under the polygons will be incorrect. This is due to the styles not being optimized for 3D.

Rotating Madness
  • VeeDefault may cause conflict with the level pack & overwrite styles.
  • All of the levels may become too fast at a certain point and impossible to avoid.
  • Exiting "To The Sky" in the middle of the level will cause the music for that level to keep playing.

Super Hexagon 2: The Unofficial Sequel (SH2:TUS)
  • "The Real End" can, at times, generate a fully closed, inescapable hexagon. This is due to RAM limitations, as mentioned above, and has been fixed in the Steam Workshop version of this level pack for modern versions of Open Hexagon.

Super Hexagon 2: The Unofficial Sequel (SH2:TUS)

Originally made as a response to a different "Super Hexagon 2" level pack made by a different creator, this level pack is my own personal interpretation of what a sequel to the original Super Hexagon would be like.

While half of the level aesthetics are continued from Super Hexagon's "Hyper" stages, it does feature its own spin on later aesthetics as you progress through the levels.

The key difference in Super Hexagon 2 compared to its original version is that in order to pass/beat a level, you need to survive for 90 seconds rather than the original game's 60.

This level pack also features songs from Bossfight as a nod to the original default level packs of Open Hexagon.
