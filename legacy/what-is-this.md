# Legacy pages

In this folder you will find all of my legacy pages from the old [Weebly website](https://huuishuu.weebly.com/).

All of the pages and their contents here are completely unused in the current version of the website, as they've been superceded by newer pages, specifically:
- Personal Blog now links to [my Bearblog page instead](https://huuishuu.bearblog.dev/blog/)
- Website Updates now links to the [update history of this GitHub repo]()
- News (old) has been unused even on Weebly itself since 2016 and has been hidden from navigation for the past 10+ years.

These pages are merely just sitting here in a permanently archived state and are no longer maintained or updated.
This also includes the RSS feed and my old "Deleted Maps" page for housing my then-deleted osu! maps. All of them since have been re-uploaded directly back to osu! and are accessible via [my osu! profile](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/6044237).

Feel free to peruse and browse these at your own leisure, but I likely won't be updating any of these to fix anything. Issues/pull requests relating to these will simply be ignored, unless they break other parts of the website for whatever reason.
