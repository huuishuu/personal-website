huu's unfinished osu! projects

This is a page dedicated to housing all of my never-finished osu! mapping projects that I've started over the years but never got around to completing.

This includes things that were simply timed and I had the idea of mapping them, to actual maps that I've started working on but never finished, either because the mapping process wasn't working out and I decided to abandon it.

If you wish to pick these up for your own mapping projects, go ahead. I am not doing anything with these sets. All I ask is to be credited.
