Considering changing the domain & regarding access issues

4/12/2024

 
Hi. It's huu again.

It's been a little while since I relaunched SHfOH. One of the biggest pet peeves I've had with the relaunch though, is not the fact that I can't design a website worth crap, even with a website builder tool like what I'm using right now, but it's primarily the domain.

Right now, the domain for the website is "shfoh.weebly.com". This poses a few problems and questions for people, such as "what the hell does shfoh mean", and most people are very turned off by the idea of seeing "Weebly" as a subdomain.

Likely because a lot of, let's say, bad actors tend to use website builders, much like Weebly, to do shady things online. Scam sites, phishing, etc. - you get the idea. For this reason, a challenge I've faced with my site is that a lot of websites block anything related to the Weebly subdomain. An example would be Steam - if I try to link my site anywhere on Steam, it'll immediately flag it as a dangerous website and even risk banning my account. Already had that happen once, actually, kinda (one of my community posts were taken down by Valve for having this site linked).

I say that, yet I tested just now by sending this website's URL to one of my alt. accounts & even linked it on my profile, neither were filtered out. Perhaps Valve lifted their spam filter on Weebly? I know it used to be a thing in the past but it doesn't seem like it is an issue right now. Can't say for certain for how long though, and I'd ideally love to just avoid it entirely.

Secondly, what does "SHfOH" mean? Well, I've explained it in an old news post but it basically means "Super Hexagon for Open Hexagon". It's an homage to what I made this website for originally. However, since my focus on this site has shifted wildly from that, I don't really think it's accurate to call it that anymore.

I'm considering changing the website's domain, ditch "SHfOH" for good, and perhaps just make it my own personal thing. I was thinking of grabbing a domain but then checked Weebly's pricing - $145/year doesn't...seem worth it to me? I don't know, really. If anyone can clarify things like this for me, get in touch with me. You'll find my contacts here.

Also, one more thing I wanna mention - I've received reports that CIS countries seemingly cannot access Weebly whatsoever, including this website. This includes countries such as Russia, Ukraine, Georgia, Armenia, etc. I've tested this myself with Windscribe and I've been able to reproduce this as well. Unfortunately though, that is out of my control. I've tried looking around in the website's console to see if I can remove these restrictions but there doesn't seem to be an option for this. Looks like a blockage on Weebly's end as a whole and not something the end-user can resolve, which sucks.

That's about all I wanted to talk about for now. If anyone has ideas on how I can improve this website just...in general - layout-wise, design-wise, etc. - please do let me know. I'd really love to hear advice on design and everything. Even for me this website is a little bit of a nightmare to navigate but at the same time - I don't know how to really make it better...

- huu

An update to the osu! section

3/30/2024

 
Hi, it's huu again.

Over the last few weeks, I've done a ton of mapper outreach in osu!, offering them some deleted maps from my collection back to them for reupload. A couple have replied, took the maps & reuploaded them, which is great!

A lot of them, however, were unresponsive, or said they didn't want/need their own maps and don't plan to reupload them. I'm personally of the opinion that content of all types deserves to be preserved, within moderation of course. Which is why I find it to be a huge shame that osu! mappers delete their own work from osu!

On one hand, I get it. You're not happy with your own content. You think your map is "subpar" and that "you can do much better". I get that, trust me. But I don't agree with deleting the maps outright, preventing it from being played by people who might see value in said maps.

That's why I've gone through my osu! folder, wrote "status=n" to filter out unsubmitted maps, and whatever popped up that hasn't previously been uploaded on this site from back in 2016-2017, I've uploaded it myself.

With this change, I've also changed the disclaimer on the osu! page to account for two things:
- Mappers wanting to take downloads of the map down
- Copyright holders wanting to take downloads of the map/song down

Check out all the new additions in the Removed osu! beatmaps page! Let me know by contacting me on osu! if you have any questions, want to add something to the list, or if you're a mapper - to take one of your maps down, just keep in mind the conditions I've laid out in order for me to acknowledge your takedown request.

That's all for now. See you!
- huu

SHfOH is...back?

3/11/2024

 
Hi there. It's been quite a while, hasn't it? I'm sure a majority of people don't even remember that this website used to exist, but welcome or welcome back to those who do remember.

As of March 11th, 2024, I've decided to bring back the SHfOH website back online. The reason is simple - history. This website contains a lot of history for me personally, with me actively maintaining it from 2014 to 2017 with all sorts of junk.

With this resurrection of the site, however, I have removed the following pages:
- osu!
- Need for Speed: World

osu! content does not need to be mirrored, as we have great mirrors in 2024 such as BeatConnect, and many many others. I've also mirrored most of my own beatmaps either by reuploading some of them back onto my osu! profile, or by putting them all on MEGA, which you can find linked here.

As for Need for Speed: World - the content I had hosted on here was DMCA'd by EA a few years back, so the old downloads I used to have are not available anymore. Plus, Soapbox Race World is a thing that exists now, so the game is very readily accessible yet again.

I have, however, added back the page for Hexis, considering that Hexis has been offline for a number of years now. I have updated the YouTube video associated with it with my own video on Hexis, considering that this website went un-updated for a number of years, the related video seems to have been taken down by the uploader too.

One more thing about the Open Hexagon stuff. 2 of the level packs that have been hosted here have actually received ports to the Steam version of Open Hexagon, which you can find linked in the text on the left! The packs that have been ported are:
- Boson X for Open Hexagon
- Super Hexagon 2 - The Unofficial Sequel

You can click the hyperlinked text above to be taken directly to the workshop pages for both packs! Shoutout to VindowVipre for helping with the porting process! I might consider further updates to both packs in the future, as I've replayed them recently and kinda dislike how basic both of them are. Especially Boson X for Open Hexagon, which could really be improved by utilizing some of version 2 & above's features.

What this also means is that the packs hosted on SHfOH are for version 1.92 only, which you can find a download for here. I will also link the same GitHub page in the page for Open Hexagon. These are the same packs that have been hosted here previously with no major changes.

That is all. I don't really plan on maintaining this site very much, but I've at least decided that I'm bringing it back online for historical preservation, especially of my old Open Hexagon packs that nowadays are hard to find, even for me. I do not plan to update Rotating Madness & Super Hexagon for Open Hexagon (SHfOH) to the Steam release of Open Hexagon.

EDIT (2024-03-11 @ 23:18 UTC+2):
After skimming through the osu! beatmap page, I've decided to bring back the page for removed osu! maps, as I do think there's genuine merit to having that page. Some of the maps on there, despite having mirrors available on Beatconnect, are not available anywhere else, particularly some maps that were never even submitted to begin with.

I feel like having a mirror for them is useful for historical preservation, so I have brought the page back and brought the osu! category back with it. I might look through my collection in the near future and actually comb through my osu! folder to find any other deleted/removed maps that are either very difficult or impossible to obtain anymore, and maybe update the website. Watch this space, I guess!

- huu
