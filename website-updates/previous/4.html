Weekly Update #5

3/11/2017

 
Hey all. Hope you had a nice week and have some plans for the weekend unlike me.
Regardless, let's get into our weekly SHfOH updates!

#1:
The Safari Biathlon Racer download has been updated to remove an "infected" file.
I myself, as well as some of you visitors have noticed that the download of Safari Biathlon Racer triggered a virus detection on various anti-viruses like Avast & Avira.
Fortunately, I have identified the cause of the detections, being a really poorly written config file that doesn't specify what directory it's allowed to modify the files in, thus triggering the detections.
The file wasn't essential for the game to run, so it has been removed.
If you continue getting virus detections on your antivirus, please let me know and I'll figure something out! Thank you.

#2:
New osu! beatmap added - Cyber-Rainforce - Fortress (Nath's Theme from 100% Orange Juice)
While this beatmap isn't quite the quality I wanted it to be when I started mapping it, I at least finished the map, so...you know. Hope you can find some enjoyment in that repetitive thing.

#3:
Updated Mousou Katharsis with a new difficulty from mcwc2307.
Nothing big this time, just a new difficulty addition and that's about it.

That's it for this week's updates.
I know, it's pretty small, but honestly, I didn't have much on my plate this week, hence the somewhat small amount of updates.

I'm not just gonna leave it here though, as I do have future plans for SHfOH.

One of my future plans is to start putting up game reviews on the site!
This isn't a final decision yet, as I'm unsure how often I'd be writing reviews, but this is part of my plans somewhat.

The next is guides! Specifically speedrunning guides.
Yes, I know websites like speedrun.com have guides written down for games, I want to write some guides personally. And by that, I mean guides written from scratch, without using any other guide as a base for anything. However, just like the reviews thing - this isn't final and I'm not sure how far I'm willing to take this.

Besides that, hope you all have a wonderful rest of the week and see you next Saturday!

Regarding Weebly & general website feedback

1/4/2017

 
Over the last few days, there have been people who have been criticizing me for using Weebly to make this website.
While I don't blame people for having distrust in Weebly as a service, I feel like I need to step in with this.

First of all - I whole-heartedly believe Weebly is a great service.
While yes, they can and should police illegitimate, fake & outright scam websites a lot better, the freedom they still provide for their Free users is still, in my opinion, very open.
Even if you don't pay a cent to Weebly, they still give you a lot of options work with, and the options they don't give you - well, they're mostly minor things that I think only a small percentage of people would actually use.

Second - I run a legitimate website here.
Whether you trust this website or not, regarding it being hosted on Weebly, I still run a legitimate website. I provide the content I make and people themselves submit for free, and for the content that isn't mine that's hosted here - remember - I have no problem taking it down, should the owner of said content not want it hosted on my website.
I have never charged anyone for any of the downloads on this site, nor do I plan to do so.
Moreover - I had only a single incident reported where a download was infected with malware. And even then, that was resolved fairly quickly, as it was a slight mistake on the file hosting service I use (being MediaFire). While I couldn't restore the download in question, as MediaFire doesn't reverse their file takedowns and me losing the original files to begin with, I still left a mirror to the official website where you can download said files from, so me hosting it on my website was pretty pointless to begin with.

Third - I do not take donations for anything this website does.
If you've noticed, there's not a single page that has a "Donate" button. Again, this website is hosted on Weebly, which is a free service, meaning I do not have to pay any bills for it. However, this does come at a slight cost - the website must have .weebly.com at the end of it. This isn't really a bother for me honestly, but this does mean my website gets filtered out on other websites & services, like Steam, because, well, there are a lot of illegitimate and scam websites hosted on Weebly, which I think Weebly should police more.

You might have a question though...
"Why not just buy a custom domain"
While I would love to, certainly, I think "shfoh.com" would look infinitely better than "shfoh.weebly.com", there's always that eternal issue of money. As you probably guessed - I don't have money.
And no, I don't want to take donations for this website.
See, I'm still a minor with no job. And that means I cannot afford pretty much anything on the internet, unless it's spoonfed to me. It's why I usually turn to free services, because you don't need to pay and they are hassle-free most of the time. Maybe when I get out of school and get an actual job I'll buy a custom domain for this site, because I do care about SHfOH. But for the foreseeable future - the website will remain with the .weebly.com thing attached at the end, as well as Weebly branding at the bottom.

"Why don't you want to take money for this website"
Because it doesn't feel right to do so.
While yes, I do host my own content here, keep in mind - I also host content that other people make.
And some of the hosted content in question is also copyrighted, such as the music inside Open Hexagon level packs and osu! beatmaps.
While yes, some could argue that this is piracy, at least regarding osu! - the content originates from osu! and all I do is just mirror it. But like I said - should owners of said content want it taken down - I will take it down as soon as I can.
Do you get my point While I did make Open Hexagon level packs and osu! beatmaps, certain elements in them are not mine, primarily the music. It doesn't feel right to take any kind of monetary compensation for them, be it donations or if I charged you, say, $4.95 for every download. It'd be wrong. And I wouldn't let myself do that, let alone the owners of the original content.

Quote me on this and remember it for as long as this website is up:
I will never take any kind of money for the work I put into this website. No matter what. This website will strictly remain free.

That said though, I do realize that some people have legitimate criticism towards the website, maybe towards its design, management or whatever.
Which is why I have added the "Feedback" page.
On that page you will find a form which you can fill out, giving your overall opinion about the website and potentially suggest improvements and whatnot.

It's something that should've been here since day one of the website, but I never added because I didn't think anyone would use it.
Granted, that fear is still there, but as this website grows - it's better to prepare for the future now than later.

That said though, I still thank those who provoked this kind of discussion.
Whether you have a positive opinion about this website or not - I'm at least happy you have some kind of opinion.

-Site Owner
