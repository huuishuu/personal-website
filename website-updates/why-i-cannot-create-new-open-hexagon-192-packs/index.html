Why I Cannot Create New Open Hexagon (1.92) Packs + Site Addition

5/29/2016

 
Over the last year, after cancelling my (at the time) unnamed level pack, which I hoped to release about 6-10 levels for, I have been very seriously considering returning to coding for Open Hexagon again. However, after my computer had died in September 2015 and moving over to a far weaker Pentium Dual-Core laptop, I have started to question if this was even possible or not.

Well, today, after redownloading Open Hexagon v1.92 on my Pentium Dual-Core and releasing an update to Boson X for Open Hexagon, I can wholeheartedly say that I am unable to.

The reason is simple - framerate.
My current processor is (for some reason) too weak to handle Open Hexagon properly, causing various glitches while playing, mainly inescapable walls and the triangle phasing into the hexagon in the middle and killing me, which have happened countless times.
That's the kind of common thing that happens while playing on my laptop.

But I mentioned it's because of the framerate
Yes.
You wanna know the framerate I get when playing Open Hexagon at all times
13 FPS. That's how much I always get when playing OH.
Because of that, the game is completely unplayable, because this royally screws up many timings, desyncs music from sync-related levels, such as Dragon Mayhem & all of my packs and makes the game literally impossible because of the issues I showed in the above images.

So, sadly, I will not be returning to making level packs for Open Hexagon v1.92. The reason I updated Boson X is because the pack itself is already fully complete and just needed a few additions and 1 fix, both of which were actually plausible, so I updated it.

Hope you understand.

However, I am now officially open to host other people's level packs here!
You may have noticed 3 new pages cropping up, being "Community Packs", "Community Updates" and "Submit".
"Community Packs" is a page where your level packs will be hosted! "Community Updates" are your level packs' changelogs! If you submit an update to an already-submitted level pack, the changelog that you include will be put in "Community Updates" for everyone to know what has changed!
Lastly, "Submit" is pretty simple - it's a page with a contact form for submitting your level packs! I will take a look at the submitted level pack, check if it works properly and if it meets at least that - the pack shall be hosted on the website!

While I'm not expecting any submissions, it's still worth a shot, I guess!
-Site Owner
