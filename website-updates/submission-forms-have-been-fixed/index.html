Submission forms have been fixed!

11/2/2016

 
Hey guys. Submission forms for osu! beatmaps & Open Hexagon level packs have been broken for a while and it's something I never noticed because no one ever really bothered to report the issue. That is, no one, except for a person named shivaco.

He pointed out to me that the submission form for osu! beatmaps was broken, so I quickly rushed to set up a warning that the form is glitched. Today, I fixed the issue, meaning the form is functional again and you can submit your beatmaps again!

I've also tested with the Open Hexagon submission form and apparently that was glitched too. So I fixed that.

To those who wanted to submit your beatmaps/level packs to the site, please re-send your submissions now that the forms have been fixed.

I'm deeply sorry for all the inconvenience and thank you once again to shivaco for reporting the issue to me!
If you guys find any more website issues, please don't hesitate to report them to me, I'll see about fixing them!
-Site Owner
