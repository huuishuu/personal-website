Weekly Update #10

4/22/2017

 

Hey guys.  We're back with another weekly SHfOH update! Our 10th one, as a matter of fact.
This week most of the content is gonna be provided by huu, as shivaco only has one change that he made to the website as a whole.

Let's begin!
huu:
#1: Added Hexis to the website!
You may recognize this gem of a game that I reviewed in a video that I made a few months back. Just a couple of days ago, on April 19th, the Hexis website has officially gone down, killing off the entire project alongside any/all beatmaps that were ever made for Hexis. And since I still had Hexis installed from the video I had made a few months ago, I decided to upload the Hexis install that I had to the website, so that people could still try it out if they are curious. The Hexis install contains 10 beatmap sets, each with 1 to 5 difficulties, so you'll at least have something to play and test your waters out with Hexis.

#2: The email of the website has been changed!
After more than a year, I finally changed the email for this website to something more reliable. Got rid of the old inbox.lt domain that I used to advertise on this very website and replaced it with a Gmail one. Let's hope things go as planned!

#3: Minor change - fixed some text on the website
Not much else has been done over the week on the website itself other than the new Hexis page appearing and some text being fixed.

shivaco:
Almost nothing this week.

#1: Removed DM Ashura - deltaMAX.

That's everything we have for you this week.
Hopefully we'll see you next week with more content!
Until then, have a good week.
