11/16/2016

 
Hey all. I would just like to inform you that submissions and updates for both osu! beatmaps and Open Hexagon level packs are going to be slowed down for around the next month or so, due to personal struggles in real life that aren't giving me time to go through & fulfill community requests.

Cheers.
