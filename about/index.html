huu's personal website

About this website

This website was first launched on June 20th, 2014, originally known as "SHfOH", its original intentions were to merely house the "Super Hexagon for Open Hexagon" mod and serve as a central hub for updates & news regarding the level pack's future.

The website has since expanded to housing other forms of content, namely now hosting content for specific, abandoned games that are difficult to find anywhere on the internet, other Open Hexagon level packs created by me & osu! content, both user-generated & my own.

On April 30th, 2024 - this website officially changed its name from "SHfOH" to "huu's personal website" in order to align it more with the website's goals of just being a controlled, personal space and as an archival project of sorts.

As of October 5th, 2025 - this website officially migrated all of its content from Weebly to GitHub in order to help better preserve this website and all of its contents.
