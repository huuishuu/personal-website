Deleted osu! beatmaps

This page houses old, removed beatmaps from osu! that have either been taken down by the mapper themselves or have never been submitted, are generally finished but were circulating in niche circles such as public Discord servers only.

If the game mode is not mentioned in the "Spread" text, the game mode is osu! (standard).

To mappers looking to get these taken down - please contact me on osu! and drop me a message stating that you want one of your maps taken down from the list. My only condition is that you resubmit the map yourself.
The whole point of this is to preserve these maps so that people still have a way to access them.

To anyone wishing to contribute to the listings below - please contact me on osu! and we'll see what you've got that can be added here.
Previously I used to have a form that you would fill out to submit stuff for the site, but that got flooded with spam, so I had to get rid of it.
