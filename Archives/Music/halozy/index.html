What is Halozy

Halozy is/was a Touhou Project doujin circle formed in 2009. Active until 2017, the circle comprised of sumijun and ノヴィタ (Novita). They are most well-known for their Trance rearrangements of Touhou themes, as well as more experimental songs.

Their songs frequently feature guest vocalists such as 美里 (Misato), ななひら (Nanahira) and emaru from the band macaroom.

On September 3rd, 2017, sumijun announced that Halozy would be going on an indefinite hiatus as of October of that year, citing being unable to make music they are satisfied with anymore, as well as the circle becoming "too commercialized" on top of personal life reasons.

In 2020 as a response to the ongoing COVID-19 lockdowns at the time, Halozy began reuploading their old songs onto their official YouTube channel with the hashtag #StayHome. This continued until April 25th, 2020 with their final upload being their song of Temptation Index from the album "FUTURE LOVE" before suddenly stopping and subsequently renaming the channel from "Halozy Official Channel" to "null".

On November 6th, 2023, Halozy officially put their entire discography on Spotify, as well as other music streaming platforms such as Apple Music, marking the first substantial re-release of their music since 2020.

Notably, the additions on these streaming platforms does not include Halozy's first album "H -Heart and Beat Technology-" and does not include any of the "Halozy Best" series of albums, however everything from 2009 to 2016 is available. It is not known as to why these albums are missing.
It is currently unknown whether or not Halozy will come out of hiatus.
