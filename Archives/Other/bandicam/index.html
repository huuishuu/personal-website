Bandicam

Bandicam is a closed-source screen capture and screen recording software originally developed by Bandisoft and later by Bandicam Company that can take screenshots or record screen changes.
Back in 2012, under the username "SonicFan6124", I translated this software into Lithuanian, which then got accepted by Bandisoft and added as an official software translation.
That original translation, as you can imagine, was not very good. In fact, it was awful - full of grammatical errors and with some strings not even being translated whatsoever.
I made a YouTube video exploring my old terrible Bandicam translation from back then, which you can watch for your entertainment here:
During that same segment where we looked at the outdated translation, I also decided to do a full re-translation of it, including the things that I didn't translate whatsoever back then.
With the help of one of my close friends - @reminalol (Twitter), we documented the entire re-translation process, which you can also watch here:
If you want to get your hands on this "modern remaster" of my old Bandicam translation, you can download it directly here:

Keep in mind that the only version of Bandicam this translation will fully work with is version 1.8.4.283, released back in July 2012 or earlier.
I'm honestly not sure if this will work with newer versions of the software, as I have not tested or verified it beyond this version, so please proceed at your own risk if you decide to test it with later releases of Bandicam.
You can grab yourself the exact copy of Bandicam where the translation has been verified to work through archive.org here:
Download (archive.org)
After around late 2013 or so, I completely stopped maintaining my version of the Bandicam translation, as I found it hard to keep up with the new features being added, alongside not being very confident in my translation skills at the time. I stopped replying to Bandisoft's emails when they requested me to update translations around that time, and the translation has now been taken over by a different person who I have no affiliation with.
I thought this would be a fun thing to archive and preserve, hence why it's on my website.
